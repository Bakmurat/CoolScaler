To enable automation (apply recommendations + admission mutation):

  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} . --reuse-values --set readOnly=false

The admission webhook only touches namespaces you opt in:

  kubectl label ns <your-namespace> coolscaler.sh/optimize=true
